AI made writing code cheap, but it didn't make judgment cheap. Someone just starting out can
ship a working app in a few hours; nobody gives them feedback on architecture, testing or
maintenance. Experienced developers have a different problem: there is no place to show how they
think and how they grow a team.

**Koina** brings both needs together in the same repo.

## Where we're heading

Our goal is small teams building open source projects together in six-week **seasons**: one
**mentor** (5+ years of experience), three to five **participants** and often an **idea owner**. Every
PR gets a review, and at the end the team demos its v0.1 live.

## Why start with streams?

Because opening all of this at once means doing none of it well. We're going one step at a time:

1. **Now: streams and clips.** We go live every Saturday at 20:00 (TRT), writing code and discussing topics and ideas, and share the most useful
   moments as short clips.
2. **Next: guest mentors.** Experienced developers join the stream and we look at real PRs.
3. **Later: community sessions.** Pairing hours, workshops, an idea pool, first contributions.
4. **Later: a first project.** A mentor and a small team ship one project in the open in six weeks.
5. **Later: seasons.** If the first project works, with more projects and teams.

That's why we aren't giving dates yet. When one step settles, we open the next.

## Why open source?

Because the work should be visible. Commit history, review comments and a demo recording are
stronger evidence than any line on a CV, for participants and mentors alike.
